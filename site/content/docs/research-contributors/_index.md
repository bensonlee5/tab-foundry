or broader model capability.

## Use This Route When

- you need to understand the active architecture and what is frozen vs active
- you need to inspect or execute a sweep without losing attribution
- you are relating synthetic-data work to benchmark or corpus questions
- you are framing broader model-capability work against the roadmap
