Use this route when you are new to the repo or want the shortest path to the
right documentation.

## Start Here

- [Repo Overview]({{< relref "/docs/getting-started/repo-overview.md" >}}):
  what the repo does, how the pipeline fits together, and the quickstart.
- [Glossary]({{< relref "/docs/getting-started/glossary.md" >}}): shared
  vocabulary for artifacts, sweeps, and model surfaces.
- [Problem Formulation]({{< relref "/docs/getting-started/problem-formulation.md" >}}):
  mathematical statement of the dagzoo prior and sandwich objectives.
- [Contributing]({{< relref "/docs/getting-started/contributing.md" >}}):
  how to prepare bounded changes for review.

## Choose Your Path

- Need commands, artifacts, export bundles, or verification guidance?
  Go to [ML Engineering]({{< relref "/docs/ml-engineering/_index.md" >}}).
- Need architecture, sweeps, or synthetic-data context?
  Go to [Research]({{< relref "/docs/research-contributors/_index.md" >}}).
- Need package ownership, planning, or config references?
  Go to [Development]({{< relref "/docs/development/_index.md" >}}).

## First 15 Minutes

1. Read [Repo Overview]({{< relref "/docs/getting-started/repo-overview.md" >}}).
1. Skim [Glossary]({{< relref "/docs/getting-started/glossary.md" >}}).
1. Choose either [ML Engineering]({{< relref "/docs/ml-engineering/_index.md" >}})
   or [Research]({{< relref "/docs/research-contributors/_index.md" >}}).
1. Use [Contributing]({{< relref "/docs/getting-started/contributing.md" >}})
   before editing code, docs, or sweep state.
